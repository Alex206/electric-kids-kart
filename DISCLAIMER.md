# Engineering and safety disclaimer

This repository is a design aid for an experimental, self-built vehicle. It is not a type-approved vehicle design, structural certification, brake certification, electrical safety approval, or substitute for inspection by a qualified vehicle engineer.

The builder remains responsible for material selection, weld quality, component compatibility, electrical protection, testing, maintenance, supervision and compliance with applicable law. Components from marketplace sellers can have incomplete or inaccurate specifications; verify critical dimensions and ratings before use.

Children must not participate in energized testing, welding, grinding, battery assembly or high-current wiring. Operation is limited to a closed, controlled area with a responsible adult, suitable protective equipment and no bystanders in the test path.
