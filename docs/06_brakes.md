# 06 — Braking system

## Required configuration

- Hydraulic foot-operated disc brake on the rear axle.
- Disc diameter approximately 180–220 mm, selected as a matched kit with caliper and master cylinder.
- Pedal ratio initially 4.5:1 to 5.5:1.
- Mechanical pedal stop prevents master-cylinder over-travel.
- Independent return spring and a brake switch that inhibits throttle.
- Regenerative braking optional and supplemental only.

## Design load

At a design mass of 160 kg and 25 km/h:

- Kinetic energy is approximately 3.86 kJ.
- A target deceleration of 0.45 g requires approximately 706 N total tyre braking force.
- With a 150 mm tyre radius, rear-axle braking torque is approximately 106 N·m.
- The theoretical stopping distance at 0.45 g is approximately 5.46 m, excluding reaction time.

## Acceptance targets

- 8 km/h: controlled stop with no pull, lock-up or pedal fade.
- 15 km/h: repeat five stops without growing pedal travel.
- 20 km/h: stop within 4.5 m on dry level pavement, excluding reaction time.
- 25 km/h: stop within 6.5 m on dry level pavement, excluding reaction time.
- Parking/holding brake, if fitted: hold the loaded kart on a 15% gradient.

These are project acceptance targets, not a statement of regulatory certification.

## Installation checks

- Brake disc runs true and cannot contact the chain or guard.
- Caliper bracket is rigid and mounted in double shear where practical.
- Flexible hose cannot rub tyres, chain, steering or sharp edges.
- Master cylinder can be bled at its highest point.
- Brake pedal remains reachable in the smallest-driver position.
- The kart can be pushed with no significant pad drag after release.

## When front brakes become necessary

Add a matched front-brake system before increasing speed beyond 25 km/h, operating on meaningful slopes, materially increasing mass, or using high-grip tyres that can overload a rear-only brake. A front system must be balanced to avoid front-wheel lock and steering loss.
