# 03 — Frame design

## Default geometry

| Parameter | Default | Adjustable range |
|---|---:|---:|
| Overall length | 1,600 mm | 1,500–1,700 mm |
| Main frame width | 760 mm | 720–820 mm |
| Overall tyre width | 920 mm | determined by hubs/wheels |
| Wheelbase | 1,100 mm | 1,050–1,180 mm |
| Front track | 820 mm | component-dependent |
| Rear track | 780 mm | component-dependent |
| Ground clearance | 60–75 mm | tyre-dependent |
| Seat travel | 250 mm | minimum |
| Pedal-box travel | 250 mm | minimum |

## Material proposal

- Main rails and crossmembers: S235JR square tube, 30 × 30 × 2 mm.
- Seat/pedal rails and light bracing: 25 × 25 × 2 mm.
- Motor/jackshaft mounting plate: 5–6 mm steel with slotted adjustment.
- Steering and axle brackets: 4–6 mm steel, mounted in double shear where practical.
- Battery enclosure: 1.5 mm steel or 2 mm aluminium with insulated liner, drainage and a vent path away from the driver. Do not make a pressure-tight box.

Do not use unidentified thin decorative tubing. Confirm the existing steel profile and wall thickness before incorporating it.

## Welding sequence

1. Build a flat jig and mark a centreline.
2. Cut, deburr and solvent-clean all tube ends.
3. Clamp the two longitudinal rails and structural crossmembers; verify both diagonals differ by less than 2 mm.
4. Tack only, alternating corners to limit distortion.
5. Install temporary axle and spindle datum bars before completing welds.
6. Weld short opposing sections rather than one continuous side.
7. Allow natural cooling; do not quench.
8. Re-check diagonal, wheelbase and axle parallelism.
9. Add seat, pedal, motor and battery brackets only after the driver-fit mock-up.
10. Grind only where a bracket must sit; do not cosmetically remove structural weld throat.

## Structural acceptance checks

- No visible undercut, porosity, incomplete fusion or cracked tack welds.
- Rear bearing brackets aligned so a bare axle slides through all bearings without force.
- Steering brackets in double shear or with a documented equivalent.
- Battery retained in all three axes by metal structure, not straps alone.
- No sharp tube ends or protruding fasteners in the driver envelope.
