# 08 — Build sequence

## Phase A — Measurement and procurement freeze

1. Complete `docs/11_measurements_needed.md`.
2. Select actual axle, hubs, front spindles, steering joints and brake kit.
3. Update `calculations/defaults.json` and the CAD parameters.
4. Place the actual seat, pedals and children in a non-moving wooden/steel mock-up.
5. Freeze wheelbase, track, seat travel and pedal travel before cutting the main frame.

## Phase B — Rolling chassis

1. Fabricate and tack the flat main frame.
2. Fit rear bearing brackets using a straight alignment shaft.
3. Mock up front spindles and steering; establish stops.
4. Fit seat and pedal sliders.
5. Complete structural welds using the sequence in `docs/03_frame.md`.
6. Assemble wheels and confirm free rolling, toe and axle alignment.

## Phase C — Brake before motor

1. Install the complete hydraulic brake system.
2. Bleed and inspect it.
3. Perform push tests with an adult pushing the kart.
4. Perform gravity/coast tests on a very gentle controlled slope with no motor installed.
5. Do not install traction power until the mechanical brake passes.

## Phase D — Drivetrain

1. Fit motor and jackshaft plates with slotted adjustment.
2. Align sprockets and chains.
3. Verify chain clearance at all frame and axle positions.
4. Fabricate full guards.
5. Rotate the axle manually through several turns and inspect for tight spots.

## Phase E — Electrical

1. Mount battery within the frame and protect all terminals.
2. Install fuse, disconnect, precharge and contactor.
3. Route traction and signal wiring separately.
4. Add strain relief at every moving or vibrating component.
5. Validate polarity and insulation with the battery disconnected.
6. Energize first through a current-limited/precharge process with drive wheels raised.

## Phase F — Commissioning

Follow `docs/09_commissioning.md` without skipping speed stages. Every change to gearing, controller, battery, brake or tyre requires relevant tests to be repeated.
