# 09 — Commissioning and test plan

## 1. Static mechanical inspection

- Frame square and welds inspected.
- Steering fasteners retained and paint-marked.
- Full steering travel without interference.
- Brake firm, leak-free and capable of holding the kart.
- Chains aligned, tensioned and fully guarded.
- Battery and motor cannot move under vigorous manual force.

## 2. Electrical tests with rear wheels raised

- Correct pack voltage and polarity.
- Fuse and disconnect installed.
- Precharge works; no severe connector spark.
- Key off means no controller enable.
- E-stop drops contactor.
- Brake switch inhibits torque.
- Accelerator returns to idle every time.
- Disconnect accelerator signal: controller must fault or command zero.
- Remote-control loss inhibits torque.
- Direction correct and reverse disabled.

## 3. Adult rolling tests

1. Limit speed to walking pace and battery current to approximately 10–15 A.
2. Drive straight for 10 m; brake to rest.
3. Repeat left/right low-speed turns and inspect steering self-return and tyre clearance.
4. Increase to 8 km/h and perform ten stops.
5. Inspect all fasteners, chains, brake temperature and electrical connections.
6. Increase to 15 km/h; repeat handling and five-stop fade test.
7. Increase to 20 km/h and measure stopping distance.
8. Only after all inspections pass, set the governed adult maximum to 25 km/h and repeat braking/thermal tests.

## 4. Thermal test

- Ten minutes of repeated acceleration and braking at 15 km/h.
- Record motor, controller, battery and cable temperatures immediately after the run.
- No connector, cable or terminal should be too hot to touch; use measured limits from component datasheets rather than touch alone.
- Investigate smell, discoloration, intermittent faults or unexpected current reduction.

## 5. Child introduction

- First child session in 8 km/h mode only.
- Parent walks beside the kart with remote inhibit/E-stop access.
- Straight-line start/stop drills before turns.
- The 5-year-old remains in the lowest mode until consistent braking and steering are demonstrated over multiple sessions.
- The 13-year-old advances to 15 km/h only after the same drills.

## Session log

Record date, driver, mode, surface, run time, maximum temperature, faults, stopping distance and maintenance performed. A template is included in the spreadsheet.
