# Contributing

1. Record every dimensional change in `calculations/defaults.json` and update the generated drawings.
2. Do not merge a change that increases maximum speed, current or battery voltage without updating the hazard analysis and commissioning tests.
3. Critical dimensions require a drawing or measurement reference; avoid freehand-only fabrication instructions.
4. Purchasing links are snapshots. Add the retrieval date and retain the required electrical/mechanical specification separately from the product name.
5. Mark unverified assumptions with `TBD` rather than treating them as facts.
