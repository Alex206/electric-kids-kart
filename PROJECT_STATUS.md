# Project status

## Completed in this initial design package

- Baseline vehicle architecture and operating limits.
- Parametric frame concept and cut list.
- Steering geometry guidance and mock-up procedure.
- Two-stage drivetrain concept and ratio calculations.
- Mechanical brake sizing targets and acceptance tests.
- 48 V traction-system schematic and safety chain.
- BOM with economy, recommended and premium sourcing alternatives.
- Initial VESC and Kelly controller commissioning guidance.

## Blocking measurements

- Existing wheel outside diameter and width.
- Front wheel bearing/bore and rear hub bolt pattern or keyway.
- Exact steel profile size, wall thickness and grade.
- Desired maximum driver mass and tallest seated dimensions.
- Available test area surface and maximum gradient.

## Not yet validated

- Structural finite-element analysis.
- Weld procedure qualification.
- Exact spindle, hub and brake-kit compatibility.
- Thermal performance of the selected motor/controller.
- Actual stopping distance and rollover threshold.
- Electromagnetic compatibility and ingress protection.
